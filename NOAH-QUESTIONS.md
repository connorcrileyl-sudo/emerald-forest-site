### Why we added wood rot, repairs and finish carpentry

- **Real searches, little competition.** Across NC every month:
  wainscoting 4,400, crown molding 3,600, coffered ceilings 1,600,
  porch railing 880, door frame repair 320, wood rot repair 170. When
  we checked "finish carpenter Asheville", Google showed mostly job ads
  and directories, not carpenters' own pages. A clear page can rank.
- **Repairs bring in customers.** A rotted sill or squeaky stair is a
  small, urgent job. Those customers come back later for the bigger
  work (stairs, doors, cabinets) and leave reviews.
- **AI answers.** ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews pull from pages
  that answer a plain question ("Can rotted wood be repaired?"). Each
  new page has a short Q&A written for that. Specialist skills like
  matching old trim profiles or restoring historic woodwork are exactly
  what people ask AI about, and few local sites mention them.
- **Each service gets its own page.** Google matches a search to the
  page about it. A line on a general page rarely ranks; a page called
  "Wood Rot Repair in Black Mountain" can.
